# ART BRIEF — Mallang Heroes basic slime units (for the image model)

You are the illustrator for a cute mobile tower-defense game. Read this whole brief now and keep it for the entire conversation.

## How we work
- Do NOT draw anything when you receive this brief. Just reply "Ready".
- After that, I will send messages like "Generate the image for step A spear now". Each such message means: GENERATE ONE IMAGE (an actual picture, not text, not JSON, not a description) for that step and that unit type, then stop and wait.
- Always answer those messages with the generated image only. Never answer with JSON, code, a plan or a text description.
- Never draw more than one step per command. Never put this brief, any words, letters, numbers, labels or captions into an image.
- Keep the same art style, colors and scale across every image in this conversation. When I attach images, treat them as the exact style reference.

## Style (every image)
- Cute glossy translucent jelly slime characters, soft painted shading, light from the top-left, soft dark outline, warm bright colors, Korean Joseon-dynasty military theme.
- Every character faces RIGHT in a 3/4 view.
- Background: one solid flat magenta color (#FF00FF) filling the whole image. No floor, no shadow on the ground, no gradient, no frame, no effects.
- Slimes have big glossy eyes with highlights, small determined eyebrows, rosy cheeks and a small mouth. No legs. They hold things with small stubby jelly hands.
- Leave generous empty space around every character. Weapons, plumes, tassels and gear must be FULLY inside the image — never cut off by the image edge. Keep weapons pointing up rather than sideways if space is tight.
- Every rank, including rank 1, holds the unit's weapon.
- Hats and helmets are WORN: pulled down snugly onto the jelly head with the jelly bulging slightly around the rim — never floating or perched on top. Belts wrap around the body and press slightly into the jelly.

## Ranks (the slime grows up rank by rank)
1. Rank 1 — smallest (about 70% of rank 4): a tiny round baby jelly droplet. Black felt Joseon jeollip hat (a LOW rounded crown with a very wide flat brim — not a top hat, not a fedora) and a plain white cotton sash.
2. Rank 2 — about 80%: bigger, rounder belly. The same jeollip hat with a red feather plume and a red band, and a navy cloth belt with a small gold buckle.
3. Rank 3 — about 90%: firmer pear shape with a small jelly hand. Plain dark steel helmet with a small red tassel, and a dark grey armor band with silver studs.
4. Rank 4 (general) — 100%: confident pear shape with two small stubby jelly hands. Steel general's helmet with a gold brim and a big red feather plume, and a red padded armor plate with gold studs.
